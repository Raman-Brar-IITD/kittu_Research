Author's POV••••••••••••••••••

Indian team is making a strong team to play world cup. They are collecting best players for worldcup. Now they only need on batter and their team will be complete.

Its 7 am , In living room of India team Apartment, whole team is gathered as their captain want to introduce someone to them. And they all can guess that it's probably their new team mate.

Soon, Everyone stand up as Rohit sharma with a young man walked inside living room with his suitcase.

" If i'm not wrong, then it's Shubhman gill right? The rising star batter..." Kl Rahul whispered to hardik pandya, who nodded in agreement.

Virat kohli shot them a look to keep quite and both of them immediately turn quite.

Rohit sharma walked to them with Shubhman gill walking beside him.

"Everyone... As you all have already guessed... He is our new team mate Shubhman gill...and from now on he will be living with us.." Rohit sharma said with a small smile.

Shubhman bowed his head at other players in a polite manner. One by one everyone introduced themselves to shubhman.

Virat kohli become happy when he got to know that Shubham see him as his role model. They tried to make conversation with their youngest, wanting to make him feel at ease.

Suddenly Rohit frowned a little when he realised something.

"What happened?" Virat asked when he noticed his frowned face.

"Where is Ishan?! " Rohit asked in confusion.

To which, virat just gave him a knowing look. Rohit eyes twitched when he understand virat's look meaning.

"Still asleep?" Rohit asked with a done face.

Virat chuckled before nodding his head.

"That boy!!...surya go wake him up and tell him to come here in 5 minutes.." Rohit said to Surya kumar yadav.

"Okay bhaiya..." By saying this surya left from there.

Their talks gain Shubhman's attention who was sitting on the couch silently. He stared at in direction where surya went to get Ishan. He don't know why but his heart is beating in a little fast pace.

It's like something is about to change in his life from today onwards, and he don't even know what.

Suddenly a loud shout and sound of running broke his train of thoughts. He stared at source of this chaos.

"Rohit bhaiya save me...ishu has gone mad.." Surya yelled while running here and there in living room.

And behind surya, a boy whose hight is little short and looks a little older than shubhman , chasing him with a jug of water.

" You are done once I catch you.." That short man shouted at surya while running behind him.

Shubhman became a little amused at other members unbothered expression, like they all are used to this.

"Ishan put that jug away right now" Rohit shouted at running boy.

"So this is Ishan..." Shubhman thought to himself while looking at the cute male.

Ishan immediately stop in his track and gave an innocent smile to Rohit, who had an "so done" expression on his face.

Ishan put the jug on the near table and went to virat and sit beside him.

"Why are always causing mischiefs huh?" Virat asked while smacking his head lightly.

"It's not my fault bhiaya...he started it first..see he dumped a glass of water on me while i was sleeping.." Ishan complained in a spoiled tone.

Only then shubhman noticed that Ishan's tshirt is slightly wet. While virat and rohit turn to glare at surya who immediately raised his both hand in surrender.

" I mean you can hardly blame me...all of us know how much of a heavy sleeper he is and rohit bhaiya said to get him in living room within 5 minutes... So that was the fastest mathod to wake him up..." Surya said while defending himself.

"Pfftt-" Shubhman couldn't able to control his laugh after hearing surya and ended cracking up.

Now everyone turn to look at chuckling man, only then Ishan noticed a new person. He frowned in confusion.

"Who is he rohit bhaiya?" He asked.

Shubhman almost chocked on his own spit when he heard ishan's question. I mean not bragging but still he is quite famous then how come this man don't know him?

While other members facepalmed in disbelief. They are really done with ishan's forgetful nature.

"He is shubhman gill. Don't tell me you don't know him? And now he is also a part of indian cricket team.." Hardik couldn't bear it and finally said.

"Ohh...that rising star batter...hehe i'm sorry I never focused on his face so yeah..." Ishan said while rubbing his neck in embarrassment.

Then he turn to look at Shubhman and forwarded his hand to greet him. Shubhman started at his hand then at ishan's face before shaking hands with him.

"He hands are soft unlike mine.." Shubhman couldn't help but compare mentally.

"Nice to meet you shubh.." Ishan said with a cute smile.

A small smile automatically appeared on Shubhman's face after seeing ishan's cute smile but no one noticed it.

"Nice to meet you too ishan but my name is shubhman not shubh,..." Shubhman pointed out.

"Nahh...i'm lazy to call you by your full name, so I will call you shubh only...if you mind then you can also call me ishu like other members.." Ishan said while half shrugging.

For some reason, Shubhman didn't like the idea of calling ishan by the same nickname which other member use.

"No need...i'm okay with ishan.." Shubhman said and he, himself don't know why his voice come out kind of cold.

"Oh..okay... Now can you leave my hand? You are holding it quite tightly" ishan said while pointing at their hands.

Shubhman looked at their hand and sure enough they are still holding hands, he immediately left ishan's hand. He didn't fail notice slight redness on ishan's hand.

"I'm sorry for holding your hand tightly.." Shubham apologized, clearly looking guilty.

Ishan immediately waved his hand in chill manner and said.

"It's okay no need to feel bad...i'm not made of glass...it's not hurting.." Ishan said softly well, he half lied actually it's hurting but not that much.

Shubhman looked a little hesitant to believe ishan but nevertheless nodded his head.

"Okay Everyone settle down... " Rohit said to everyone.

Everyone nodded and took their respective seat on the couch and ishan somehow ended up sitting beside Shubhman.

" So now let's talk about Shubhman's sleeping arrangement...we have space in only shreyas and Ishan's room...so with whom you want to share room?" Rohit asked to Shubhman.

"I'm okay with either of them...you can ask them.." Shubhman replied formally.

When ishan heard him, he frowned a little at his formal tone but didn't said anything as he will make sure help Shubhman in opening up with team later.

And to make it happen, they need to spend more time together. So thinking that ishan suddenly said.

"Bhaiya...shubh can shift with me in my room. It's quite boring to live alone..so let him stay in my room.."

Shubhman gave him a look but didn't said anything. Everyone present there seems to be agree with ishan.

"Okay then..it's decided shubhman will stay with ishan..And ishan go take him to your room and help him with unpacking..," Rohit said.

"Okay bhaiya... Shubh let's go.." By saying this ishan grabbed Shubhman's wrist and pulled him upstairs to show his room.

Shubhman felt something wierd when ishan grabbed his wrist but he choose to ignore it and continue to follow ishan while dragging his suitcase.

Shubhman really don't know why but he is having a wierd urge to know ishan more and he want to explore ishan's every side, layer by layer. He for the first time wants to know someone better.

"Enough with your curiosity,Shubhman. Get a grip...you are sounding like creep..." He scolded himself in mind.
